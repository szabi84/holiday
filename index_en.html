About us

Welcome to our page!

We offer apartments in Balatonlelle, one of the most beautiful towns around Lake Balaton. We have two nicely furnished, well-equipped apartments for 6-6 people, located in a quiet green area with a lovely garden and parking.

  • Quiet, green surroundings

  • Well-equipped apartments

Information about the apartments
